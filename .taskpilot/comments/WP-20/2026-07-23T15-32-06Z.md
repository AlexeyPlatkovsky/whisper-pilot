Completion evidence — route run WP-20:fix-bug:1 (task-quality: Quality gate: pass).

ROOT CAUSE. Transcription was hardcoded to Russian: meetings.rs stamped language: "ru" on every new meeting, lib.rs transcribe_meeting read it back, transcribe.rs passed it to params.set_language. Forcing a language the audio is not in makes whisper collapse onto its highest-prior training artifact for that language. A 5-minute English standup produced 11 identical segments of 'Субтитры сделал DimaTorzok', one per fixed 30s analysis window. Reproduced byte-for-byte outside the app against the same model and FullParams; changing only the language to auto gave DETECTED LANG: en and a correct transcript. Audio ingest (mean -23.8 dB of real speech) and every decode threshold (all at whisper.cpp upstream defaults; no_context already true) were ruled out with evidence.
