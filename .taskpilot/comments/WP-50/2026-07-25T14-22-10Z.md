Empirical tuning update (this session, after code-reviewer rework):

Original candidate threshold=0.95 (min_duration_on/off=1.5) was REJECTED after
discovering it causes a real SIGBUS crash in sherpa-onnx's native
fast-clustering C++ code on a real 92s recording (bisected: 0.93 safe, 0.94+
crashes on that recording). This is a genuine, input-dependent native-library
crash risk, not just a diminishing-returns tradeoff -- a SIGBUS is a fatal OS
signal, not a catchable Rust panic or Result::Err, so it would kill the whole
app process mid-transcription, bypassing apply_diarization_outcome's
fail-open guarantee entirely.

Re-tuned to threshold=0.9, min_duration_on/off=1.0 for a real safety margin
below the observed crash boundary. Validated crash-free and cluster-reducing
against 3 real recordings of different lengths (861.5s: 22->7 clusters,
92.4s: 3->1, 240s: 14->6). Only the 861.5s recording has a confirmed real
speaker count (2); the other two serve as crash-safety/reduction evidence
only, not merge-quality evidence.

Residual risk (not fully closed by this task): the chosen safety margin
(0.93 safe / 0.94+ crash boundary observed on ONE recording) is empirical,
not a proof -- a different, as-yet-untested recording could in principle hit
an unknown lower crash threshold even at 0.9. This is a known limitation of
threshold/min-duration tuning as a mitigation, not a bug in this task's own
implementation. See WP-50's Constraints paragraph (patched) and
docs/architecture.md for the durable record.
