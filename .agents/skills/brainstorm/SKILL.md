# Brainstorm

## Apply when

Use only when a decision has at least two viable choices with different trade-offs. Do not use for implementation,
routine preference selection, or a question answerable from project evidence.

## Outcome

The user chooses an option with its consequences understood.

## Policy

- State the decision and why it blocks progress.
- Present two or three materially different options.
- Compare impact on users, architecture, delivery risk, and reversibility.
- Recommend one option and state the assumption behind the recommendation.
- Ask one decision question, then stop.
- Do not edit files or treat silence as approval.

## Result

Report the selected option, rejected options, accepted trade-offs, and any follow-up scope.
